# Data attribution and rights boundary

The MIT license in this repository applies only to original project code and documentation. It does not relicense third-party data, factual source collections, trademarks, or source-site content.

## Published inputs

- **NFL Draft selection attributes:** [nflverse draft-pick releases](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/draft_picks), whose repository is labeled [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/blob/master/LICENSE.md). This project transforms the data, limits the published fields, and identifies nflverse as the source.
- **Structured identity corroboration:** [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing), whose structured data is CC0.
- **Birthplace corroboration:** [DBpedia](https://www.dbpedia.org/about/) and English Wikipedia-derived records. DBpedia documents CC BY-SA/GFDL terms. The app publishes derived county classifications, not copied biography text.
- **County boundaries and population:** [U.S. Census Bureau 2020 cartographic boundaries](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/2020/geo/cartographic-boundary-file.html) and 2020 Decennial Census population.
- **School location reference:** [NCES EDGE school locations](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/edge/geographic/schoollocations).
- **Conference reference:** [NCAA historic membership](https://ncaaorg.sidearmsports.com/sports/2024/1/4/ncaa-historic-membership-dashboard.aspx).

## Verification-only sources

[NFL Play Football](https://playfootball.nfl.com/news-events/news-and-features/where-2026-nfl-draft-picks-played-in-high-school-by-state/) and [High School Football America](https://highschoolfootballamerica.com/for-the-11th-straight-year-high-school-football-americas-nfl-draft-tracker-will-let-you-know-where-every-pick-played-in-high-school/) were used to verify player-school relationships. Their articles and tables are not republished; the public artifact contains only derived county classifications and anonymous analytical fields.

NFL and team names are used descriptively. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Football League, its clubs, or the listed source organizations.
